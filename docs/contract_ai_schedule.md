# AI Contract Schedule (template clauses)

1. **No training on customer data.** Vendor and sub-processors may not use our data, prompts, outputs or derived data to train, tune or improve any model without written opt-in.
2. **Breach and AI incident notice.** Notify within 72 hours of becoming aware, with cooperation and root-cause report within 10 working days.
3. **Model change control.** 30 days' notice of material model changes; right to re-test; right to remain on the prior version for 90 days; re-run fairness tests before release and share results.
4. **Performance and fairness warranties.** Maintain stated accuracy and fairness thresholds (impact ratio, equal-opportunity difference) with remedies and termination right on breach.
5. **Audit and documentation.** Provide model card, bias audit summaries, security reports and logs on request; allow audit on 10 working days' notice.
6. **Sub-processors and foundation models.** Disclose and flow down these terms; notify of changes.
7. **Human oversight.** System must permit human review and override of consequential outputs and must not be the sole basis of decisions with legal or similar effect.
8. **Regulatory cooperation.** Support our compliance duties (EU AI Act deployer obligations, NYC LL144 notices, DPIAs) at no extra charge.
9. **Liability and indemnity.** Indemnity for IP claims on training data and outputs, data protection breach, and discriminatory output claims; liability cap not below insured levels.
10. **Exit.** Data return in agreed format, deletion certificate within 30 days, transition assistance of 90 days.
